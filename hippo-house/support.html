Olvivlo — Support

Need help with Olvivlo? Email support@olvivlo.com and include your device and iOS version. We usually reply within a few days.

Multiplayer: online or against bots

Open Multiplayer to find a match. You can compete with other players online or play against computer-controlled hippos. The game fills empty places with bots so a match can get going. Online play needs an internet connection; local bot matches work offline. Online multiplayer is being prepared for launch.

If your connection drops during an online round, the game shows Connection lost with your standings so far. Leave returns you to the home menu so you can search again.

Daily Challenge

Choose Daily on the home menu for one level and layout shared by everyone, new each day. You get one free play a day. Extra Daily Plays are available as an optional purchase after your free play is used. Sign in to Game Center to compare your score on the Daily leaderboard.

I bought something and it didn't unlock

Purchases come back when the game starts. If a permanent item is still missing, open More → Settings on the home menu, or Settings in the pause menu, and tap Restore Purchases. Characters, buddies and shots restore on devices signed in to the same Apple Account. Time packs and Extra Daily Plays are consumable and cannot be restored after use. Unused time packs are stored on your device and are lost if the app is deleted.

Leaderboards and achievements

World and Daily leaderboards and achievements use optional Game Center sign-in. Manage your sign-in in Settings → Game Center on your iPhone or iPad, or open More → Records → WORLD in the game. Scores recorded offline can be submitted when you are online again.

Refunds

Apple handles purchases and refunds. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in and choose the purchase.

How do I play?

The hippo flies on its own and goes where you look: drag on the right side of the screen to steer, and aim up to climb. Tap SHOOT to blow bubbles, hold BOOST for speed, and hold BRAKE to stop in the air. Grab stars before the clock runs out. How to Play under More explains the modes and powerups.

Privacy

See the Privacy Policy for information shared during online play, optional Game Center features and purchases. Ads are disabled.
