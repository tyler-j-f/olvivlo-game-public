Hippo House — Support

Need help with Hippo House? Email support@olvivlo.com and include your device (for example, iPhone 15) and iOS version. We usually reply within a few days.

I bought something and it didn't unlock

Purchases come back on their own when the game starts. If one is still missing, open Settings (on the home menu, or pause during a run) and tap Restore Purchases. Characters, buddies and shots come back on any device signed in to the same Apple Account. Time packs are used up when you spend them and can't be restored; unused packs are kept on the device and are lost if the app is deleted.

Refunds

Purchases are made through Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple: go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose the purchase.

How do I play?

The hippo flies on its own and goes where you look: drag on the right side of the screen to steer, and aim up to climb. Tap SHOOT to blow a bubble at anyone chasing you, hold BOOST for speed, and hold BRAKE to stop in the air. Grab as many stars as you can before the clock runs out. The How to Play page on the home menu explains every mode and powerup.
