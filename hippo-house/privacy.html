Olvivlo — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Olvivlo is a game for iPhone and iPad made by Tyler Fitzgerald ("we", "us"). Your progress is saved on your device. Optional online multiplayer and Game Center features share information as described below. No Olvivlo account is required, and we do not sell your information.

What stays on your device

The game saves best scores, unlocked levels and modes, settings, equipped looks and owned items, including unused time packs, on your device. Deleting the app deletes this local progress. Permanent purchases can be restored through Apple with Restore Purchases in Settings.

Online multiplayer

Online multiplayer is being prepared for launch. When available, it uses Photon, a service operated by Exit Games, to connect players and relay match traffic. Photon processes connection information, such as your IP address and a connection identifier. Match traffic includes your in-game display name, equipped looks, movement, pickups, hits and scores, which other players need to see. The display name may be based on your device name and may contain a name you gave your device. There is no chat.

We do not operate our own match servers. Photon processes online connection and match information; see Photon's Privacy & Cookie Policy for its information-handling practices. Playing Single Player, Daily or a local bot match does not require a Photon connection. Optional Game Center features can still send information to Apple when you are signed in.

Game Center and Daily leaderboards

Game Center sign-in is optional. If you sign in, the game sends scores and achievement progress to Apple's Game Center and reads leaderboard entries, including Daily Challenge rankings. Other players can see your Game Center nickname, scores and rank on leaderboards. Apple handles this information under Apple's Privacy Policy. You can play without signing in and manage Game Center in your device's Settings.

In-app purchases

Apple handles purchases through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details, billing address or Apple Account credentials. Apple tells the game which items were purchased so they can be unlocked. Characters, buddies and shots are permanent items; time packs and Extra Daily Plays are consumable. Apple's handling of purchase information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Ads and analytics

Advertising and analytics are disabled in this version. The game does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites.

Your choices

You can play solo, Daily and local bot matches offline. You can stay signed out of Game Center to keep your scores off its leaderboards. In-app purchases are subject to the device's parental controls, including Screen Time and Ask to Buy.

Changes to this policy

Changes will be posted here with a new date before the version they describe is released.
