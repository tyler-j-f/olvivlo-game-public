Hippo House — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Hippo House is a game for iPhone and iPad made by Tyler Fitzgerald ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it. The short version: we collect nothing.

Information we collect

None. Hippo House does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal information. It has no account or sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs that collect data. The game does not connect to the internet for gameplay, and it does not request access to your contacts, photos, location, camera, microphone or any other part of your device.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress on your device only: best scores, unlocked levels and modes, your settings, which looks you have equipped, and which in-app purchases you own (including unused time packs). This information never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes it, except that purchases of permanent items can be restored with Restore Purchases in the shop.

In-app purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or receive your name, Apple Account, payment details or billing address. Apple tells the game only which item was bought so it can be unlocked. Apple's handling of your information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

Hippo House is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. In-app purchases are subject to the parental controls on the device (Screen Time and Ask to Buy).

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. If the game ever starts collecting any information, this page will say so before that version is released.
